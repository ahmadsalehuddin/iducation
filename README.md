# iDucation: learning that adapts to how each student learns

A concept for a school learning platform for **SDG 4, Quality Education**. It presents each chapter in the learning style a student does best in (visual, auditory or tactile) and helps teachers group students using what the platform learns about them.

Coursework for **ICT653 Technologies for Futuristic Systems**, Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons.), Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM). Group project, submitted July 2022.

## The problem

The pandemic left Malaysian pupils months behind their syllabus. When schools reopened, teachers had little time to cover the chapters and couldn't help every student keep up. One way of teaching doesn't fit every child.

## The concept

- **Sign in.** Students don't sign up. Accounts come from the Ministry of Education's records and are set up by an administrator.
- **Core subjects.** Each subject card shows its chapters, the student's progress, and a suggested learning style for the day. The suggestion (AI in the concept) finds the style a student does best in, but also rotates styles so students learn to adapt.
- **Electives.** Chosen by each school (computing, music, cooking, sport, art and more), with "Most popular", "Based on your previous activity" and "Suggested for you" picks to follow a student's interests as they change.
- **Chapters.** Each chapter shows its subtopics and whether it's done.
- **Learn, then assess.** "Start/Continue learning" shows the same chapter as a video (visual), audio (auditory) or a game (tactile), such as an addition and subtraction game for Year 1 Mathematics. "Take assessment" gives everyone the same quiz, whatever the style, and keeps the results.
- **Teacher dashboard.** Student performance, teaching hours, class size, electives, and three kinds of grouping recommendation: by performance (mixing stronger and weaker students rather than putting top students together), by learning style, and by interest.

## How we got there

Futures-thinking tools from the course: horizon scanning, a driver mapping diagram, a futures wheel, a SWOT analysis and a three-year roadmap. The idea was then built as a clickable mid-fidelity prototype in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## Why there's no code here

The prototype was built on a paid HTML template, and the licence doesn't allow sharing it. This repo describes the idea and the design thinking instead.
